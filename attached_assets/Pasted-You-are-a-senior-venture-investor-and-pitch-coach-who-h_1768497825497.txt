You are a senior venture investor and pitch coach who has sat through hundreds of founder presentations.

Build an **AI-powered Investor Pitch Preparation Module** inside my portal.

This module is NOT for building slides.
This module is for preparing founders to **deliver the pitch, handle objections, and survive live Q&A**.

It must feel like a tough rehearsal with a real investor.

---

## INPUTS (NO NEW DATA COLLECTION)

This module must consume:

* The uploaded business plan
* The finalized pitch deck content (from the pitch deck module)
* Investor Mode (Angel / Institutional VC)

Do NOT ask the user to re-enter information unless explicitly necessary.

---

## CORE OBJECTIVE

Prepare the founder to:

* Present confidently without reading slides
* Anticipate tough investor questions
* Respond clearly, calmly, and credibly
* Avoid defensive or amateur answers
* Turn objections into trust-building moments

This is **presentation readiness**, not content creation.

---

## STEP 1 — PITCH DELIVERY SCRIPT (FOUNDER VOICE)

Generate a **spoken presentation script**, slide by slide.

For EACH slide in the pitch deck, output:

```
SLIDE: [Slide Title]

What You Say (Out Loud):
(30–60 seconds of natural, spoken language — not reading bullets)

Key Emphasis:
(What investors should remember from this slide)

Delivery Tip:
(Pacing, tone, or emphasis guidance)
```

Rules:

* Conversational, confident tone
* No jargon dumping
* No reading from slides
* Optimized for live presentation

---

## STEP 2 — INVESTOR OBJECTION GENERATION

Based on the pitch deck and business plan, generate **high-probability investor objections**.

Group objections into these categories:

1. Problem & Urgency
2. Solution & Differentiation
3. Market Size & Returns
4. Traction / Proof
5. Business Model
6. Go-To-Market
7. Competition
8. Team
9. Timing / Why Now
10. Risk & Downside

For EACH objection, output:

```
Investor Objection:
(Exact wording an investor would use)

Why This Comes Up:
(The real concern behind the question)

Risk Level:
(Low / Medium / High)
```

No polite phrasing. No softening. Use real investor language.

---

## STEP 3 — OBJECTION RESPONSE SCRIPT (LIVE Q&A)

For EACH objection, generate a **live-response playbook**:

```
Best Short Answer (Live Pitch):
(1–2 sentences, calm and confident)

If They Push Further:
(Expanded explanation, max 4 sentences)

What NOT to Say:
(Common founder mistakes or defensive responses)
```

Tone:

* Direct
* Calm
* Non-defensive
* Investor-fluent

Answers must sound **spoken**, not written.

---

## STEP 4 — DEAL-BREAKER AWARENESS

For each objection, classify:

```
Objection Type:
- Clarifiable
- Needs Proof Soon
- Structural Risk
- Likely Deal-Breaker (for this investor type)

What Would Reduce This Concern:
(Specific evidence, milestone, or signal)
```

This helps founders know **when to explain vs when to move on**.

---

## STEP 5 — RAPID-FIRE PITCH REHEARSAL

Generate a **mock investor Q&A drill**:

* 10–12 questions
* One question per line
* Increasing difficulty
* No hints shown initially

Then provide a separate section with:

* Ideal answers
* Time guidance (e.g. “Answer in under 20 seconds”)

Designed for **practice out loud**.

---

## FINAL OUTPUT — PITCH PREPARATION SCRIPT

Export a clean document titled:

### “INVESTOR PITCH PREPARATION & OBJECTION PLAYBOOK”

Sections:

1. Spoken Pitch Script
2. High-Risk Investor Objections
3. Live Response Playbook
4. Deal-Breaker Awareness
5. Rapid-Fire Rehearsal Questions

Rules:

* Plain text
* Clear spacing
* No emojis
* No fluff
* Designed for rehearsal, printing, and review

---

## UI REQUIREMENTS

* Toggle: “Practice Mode” (hide answers)
* Highlight High-Risk objections
* Link objections back to pitch sections
* One-click copy / export

---

## NON-GOALS

* No slide editing
* No pitch rewriting
* No investor CRM
* No motivational coaching

This module exists to **prepare the founder for the room**, not the deck.

---

## SUCCESS CRITERIA

A prepared founder should say:
“I know what they’re going to challenge me on — and I’m ready.”

If the output feels polite, generic, or easy, the module has failed.
